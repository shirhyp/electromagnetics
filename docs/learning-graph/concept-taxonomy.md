# Concept Taxonomy

Every concept in the learning graph belongs to one of the 14 categories below.
The TaxonomyID is the short code stored in `learning-graph.csv`; the category
name is what the graph viewer shows in its legend.

| TaxonomyID | Category | What belongs here |
|---|---|---|
| FOUND | Math and Circuit Foundations | Complex numbers, sinusoids and phasors, decibels, and the circuit laws and power ideas students bring from circuit analysis. |
| VEC | Vector Analysis | Coordinate systems, differential elements, line, surface and volume integrals, gradient, divergence, curl, and the integral theorems. |
| WAVE | Wave Fundamentals | Traveling and standing waves, wavelength, phase velocity, rise time, electrical length, and when circuit theory breaks down. |
| TLINE | Transmission Line Theory | Line types, the distributed-element model, telegrapher's equations, propagation constant, characteristic impedance, losses and dispersion. |
| TERM | Terminated Transmission Lines | Reflection coefficient, standing-wave patterns and VSWR, input impedance, stubs and quarter-wave lines, and power flow to a load. |
| SMITH | Smith Chart and Impedance Matching | Reading and rotating on the Smith chart, admittance charts, and lumped-element, single-stub and quarter-wave matching. |
| TRANS | Transients and Signal Integrity | Step and pulse response, bounce diagrams, reactive-load transients, TDR, signal integrity, and termination schemes for digital interconnects. |
| ESTAT | Electrostatics | Charge distributions, Coulomb's and Gauss's laws, potential, conductors and dielectrics, boundary conditions, capacitance, and Laplace's and Poisson's equations. |
| MSTAT | Magnetostatics | Lorentz force, Biot–Savart and Ampère's laws, magnetic flux and vector potential, magnetic materials, and inductance. |
| MAXW | Maxwell's Equations | Faraday's law, displacement current, Maxwell's equations in integral and point form, time-harmonic fields, and time-varying boundary conditions. |
| PLANE | Uniform Plane Waves | The wave and Helmholtz equations, plane waves in lossless and lossy media, skin depth, polarization, and the Poynting vector. |
| REFL | Reflection and Transmission | Normal and oblique incidence, Snell's law, Fresnel coefficients, total internal reflection, and the Brewster angle. |
| WGUID | Waveguides | Rectangular waveguides, TE and TM modes, cutoff, the dominant mode, guide wavelength, and waveguide power and loss. |
| ANT | Antennas | Radiation from the Hertzian and half-wave dipoles, radiation patterns, directivity and gain, effective aperture, and the Friis equation. |
